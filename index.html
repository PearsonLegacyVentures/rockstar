✳ A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

KNOW BEFORE
YOU GO.

Need something different? Share your plans in the booking request and we'll take it from there.

Do you pick up cruise passengers?+

Yes, cruise guest tours and transportation are part of our services. Share your ship name, arrival/departure times and preferred pickup when requesting a booking. Meeting instructions are confirmed directly.

Can you collect us from a hotel or resort?+

Hotel pickup and tours in Nassau can be arranged. Let us know where you're staying and your preferred time.

Are the website prices final?+

No. The rates shown are approximate starting prices, not live inventory. Your total depends on the activity, group size, date and any extras. We confirm the final quote before payment.

Can you organize a family reunion or large group?+

Yes. Private groups and family reunions are central to Rockstar. Include your approximate headcount, ages and interests so we can discuss suitable transport and activities.

Can I book ATV rentals or swimming pigs?+

You can request either experience through Rockstar. Availability, location, operator requirements and specific inclusions will be confirmed before a booking is accepted.

Can I pay or confirm instantly on this site?+

Not yet. This is a booking-request website, not a live payment or instant inventory system. The team must confirm availability and a final price before your booking is complete.
